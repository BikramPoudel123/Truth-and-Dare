Full description

Truth reveals. Dare dares. Play the ultimate party game with friends — online!

Truth or Dare is the multiplayer party game that turns any night into an unforgettable one. Challenge a random player or invite friends with a private room code, then pick your mode and let the chaos begin.

  • Quick Match — jump into a game with a random opponent in seconds.
  • Private Rooms — create a room and share the code with your friends.
  • Truth or Dare Modes — answer bold truths or complete wild dares.
  • Photo & Video Answers — prove your dares with the camera.
  • Voice Dares — record and send voice replies.
  • Question Bank — thousands of questions, with new ones added by the community.
  • Moods — casual, funny, deep, flirty, spicy and extreme playlists.
  • Reactions — react to the best answers and level up your profile.
  • Play Style & Levels — your personality builds your reputation as a player.
  • Friends — send friend requests and stay connected after games.

Are you brave enough to answer the truth? Daring enough to do the dare? Download Truth or Dare and find out!
